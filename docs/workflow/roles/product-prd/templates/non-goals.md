## Frontier Reference Boundary / 前沿参考边界

External papers, engineering practices, and industry references do not become product scope unless explicitly confirmed by the user or supported by current project evidence.

外部论文、工程实践和行业参考不会自动进入产品范围，除非用户明确确认，或当前项目证据能够支撑。
